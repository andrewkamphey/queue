# Queue for X and LinkedIn: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Queue for X and LinkedIn ("Queue") is a Chrome extension made by Andrew Kamphey (kamphey.com).

## What Queue reads

On x.com and linkedin.com only, Queue reads the dates and times of your scheduled posts from the page, so it can pick the next free slot. It does not read the text of your posts, your messages, or your contacts.

## What Queue stores

Queue saves, in your browser only (Chrome's local extension storage):

- the dates and times of your scheduled posts
- your cadence settings (for example "every hour" or "daily at 9 PM and 1 AM")

## What Queue sends

Nothing. Queue has no server and makes no network requests of its own. It doesn't use analytics, tracking or ads. Your data is never sold, shared or transferred to anyone.

Scheduling happens through X's and LinkedIn's own schedule buttons, exactly as if you clicked them yourself.

## Removing your data

Uninstall the extension, and Chrome deletes everything Queue stored.
